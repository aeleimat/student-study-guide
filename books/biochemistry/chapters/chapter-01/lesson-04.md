# Chapter 1, lesson 4: Metabolic Classification: Glucogenic and Ketogenic

Source: pages/chapter-01/lesson-04/text.md and page_0012.png, page_0013.png, page_0014.png (slides 12-14). All three PNGs were opened. Slide 12 is a table. Slide 13 is not a figure: it is a stock photograph (see Figures and Possible problems). Slide 14 is the references.

## Concepts

1. **Metabolic classification is by metabolic fate: ketogenic, mixed glucogenic and ketogenic, glucogenic** (p12, "Based on the metabolic fate"). Stated. Medium.
2. **Ketogenic: ketone bodies only** (p12). Stated. Easy.
3. **Only two amino acids are pure ketogenic: leucine and lysine, "LL"** (p12). Stated. Easy; the mnemonic is the slide's.
4. **Mixed glucogenic and ketogenic: carbohydrate and ketone bodies, "FITTT"**: phenylalanine, isoleucine, tyrosine, tryptophan, threonine (p12). Stated. Medium; the mnemonic is "fit, with three T's" in the voice.
5. **Glucogenic: carbohydrate only. The remaining 13 are pure glucogenic**: alanine, arginine, asparagine, aspartic acid, glutamic acid, glutamine, glycine, cysteine, histidine, methionine, proline, serine, valine (p12). Stated. Medium; the slide's list is a run of names with no mnemonic.
6. **Count: 2 + 5 + 13 = 20** (p12: 2 ketogenic, 5 mixed (FITTT), 13 glucogenic). Derived from the slide's numbers; the slide does not add them. Easy.
7. **Carbohydrate means glucose** (p12, "Carbohydrate only", "Carbohydrate & Ketone bodies"). Stated as "carbohydrate". The word glucose is not on the slides; the brief uses "glucogenic → glucose". Medium.
8. **Closing sort (not on the slides)**: the brief asks for one amino acid filed under all four classifications at once (for example, lysine: aliphatic, basic, essential, ketogenic). Built from lessons 2, 3 and this one; derived, not printed.
9. **Slide 13 teaches nothing biochemical** (see Figures). The recap in the brief's plan is therefore made from slides 12 and the earlier lessons, not from slide 13.

## Vocabulary

- **Metabolic fate**: what the amino acid is turned into when it is broken down (p12).
- **Ketogenic**: gives ketone bodies (p12).
- **Glucogenic**: gives carbohydrate (p12; glucose in the brief's voice).
- **Mixed glucogenic and ketogenic**: gives both (p12).
- **Ketone bodies**: the fuel products of ketogenic amino acids (p12; named only, not explained on the slides).
- **Carbohydrate**: the glucogenic product (p12).
- **LL**: the mnemonic for leucine and lysine (p12).
- **FITTT**: the mnemonic for phenylalanine, isoleucine, tyrosine, tryptophan and threonine (p12).
- **Pure glucogenic / pure ketogenic**: the slide's own words for the two extremes (p12).

## Questions

Quick checks (side cards):
- **q1** (after `keto`): Which two amino acids are purely ketogenic? Options: leucine and lysine; phenylalanine and tyrosine; alanine and glycine; threonine and tryptophan. Answer: leucine and lysine.
- **q2** (after `count`): How many amino acids can make glucose (glucogenic plus mixed)? Options: 13; 15; 18; 20. Answer: 18 (13 + 5).

Practice (7; a picture per question):
1. Which pair is purely ketogenic? (picture: the fork with the ketone flask) Options: leucine and lysine; phenylalanine and isoleucine; alanine and glycine; serine and threonine. Answer: leucine and lysine. Why: "LL: ketone bodies only".
2. Phenylalanine is in "FITTT". What does its skeleton give? (picture: a split chip) Options: glucose only; ketone bodies only; both; neither. Answer: both.
3. Sort three (grid of 3 rows; each row picks glucogenic, ketogenic or mixed): alanine (glucogenic), leucine (ketogenic), tryptophan (mixed). Why lines: "not LL or FITTT", "LL", "a T in FITTT".
4. How many amino acids are purely glucogenic? (picture: three columns, the 2 and 5 filled, the third empty) Options: 12; 13; 15; 18. Answer: 13 (20 - 2 - 5).
5. Which of these is NOT one of the fit-with-three-T's amino acids? Options: lysine; threonine; isoleucine; tyrosine. Answer: lysine (it is "LL", ketogenic only).
6. Chapter review, find the mistake: the lysine card says aliphatic basic, basic, essential, glucogenic. Which tab is wrong? Options: structural; chemical; nutritional; metabolic. Answer: metabolic (lysine is ketogenic). Picture: the card with four clickable tabs.
7. Chapter review: which amino acid is aromatic, essential, and gives both glucose and ketone bodies? Options: phenylalanine; tyrosine; tryptophan; leucine. Answer: phenylalanine (tyrosine is non-essential, tryptophan is heterocyclic, leucine is ketogenic only).

All answers checked against the lecture lists: Leu, Lys; Phe, Ile, Tyr, Trp, Thr; the 13 others.
