# Biochemistry: chapter map

The repo's second book, made on 2026-11-27 with Biology's look, voice and models (books/biology/style.json). One unit per lecture-slide PDF (medical biochemistry lectures, in books/biochemistry/source/), numbered in teaching order. Lessons live in `chapter-NN/lesson-NN/` folders (site/, content/, books/…/chapters/, pages/, review/, tools/), as in Biology. Inside the engine a lesson's number is chapter × 100 + lesson (101); see site/biochemistry/index.html. The slides have no question pages: each lesson writes its own checks and practice. Team brief: chapters/BRIEF.md. Shared kit: site/biochemistry/lib/protein.js (with water.js, macro.js, metab.js and, for metab.js, cell.js, cyto.js and membrane.js copied from Biology).
