## Kit notes (metab.js is written; its header comment is the API)

- Internal helpers start with `q`: pages must not declare globals named `q…`.
- `energyDiagram.set({...})` and `allostericEnzyme.set({...})` take the whole pose every call; keys left out reset to defaults.
- `energyDiagram`'s ΔG bracket and label stand about 150 px right of `w`; leave room. Two E_A brackets on a diagram narrower than ~600 crowd.
- The noncompetitive inhibitor's label runs left of its socket; pass `labels: false` and draw your own where tight.
- Cross-fades on one spot (like Chapter 7's naKPump): `coupledReaction` turns the ATP spark into the ADP chip in place (k .24–.32); `atpCycle`'s token swaps spark → ADP chip as it travels.
- `substrateShape`: `.split`/`.join` and `.place` set the same half positions (last call wins); `.convert` only recolours. In `enzymeInhibition`, `.set` poses inhibitor and substrate; don't move them by hand.
- ATP hydrolysis: the Pᵢ "i" shows at k > .45; the burst runs .42–.9.
- The strip's small enzymes are closed clamps; `allostericEnzyme`'s active/inactive difference is moderate, so show the two forms side by side for contrast. `ballSlope`'s kinetic bar reads 1 − potential once the ball moves.
- Pathway strip: `.build .focus .tilt .spark .flow .feedback .pt` (see header).
- `pathwayStrip.flow()` fails the e2e replay check as is: a dot passing exactly (0, 0) loses its transform attribute and gets it back after its opacity, so attribute order differs between played and seeked frames. Workaround (lesson 3's `dotOrder(ps)`): pose every flow dot through (0, 0) once right after building the strip.
