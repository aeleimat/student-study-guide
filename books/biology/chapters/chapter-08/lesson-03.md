## Concepts

1. **The free-energy change of a reaction tells whether it occurs spontaneously** (p17). Stated; biologists need to know which reactions run by themselves and which need energy.
2. **Free energy is energy that can do work when temperature and pressure are uniform, as in a living cell** (p18). Stated.
3. **ΔG = ΔH − TΔS**: ΔH change in enthalpy (total energy), ΔS change in entropy, T temperature in Kelvin (p19). Stated; formula boxed.
4. **Only a negative ΔG is spontaneous; spontaneous processes can be harnessed to do work** (p19). Stated.
5. **Free energy is a measure of instability, a tendency to change to a more stable state** (p20). Stated.
6. **In a spontaneous change free energy decreases and stability increases** (p20). Stated.
7. **Equilibrium is maximum stability (ΔG = 0, student's ink); a process can do work only while moving toward equilibrium** (p20).
8. **Exergonic: net release of free energy, spontaneous, ΔG < 0. Endergonic: absorbs free energy from the surroundings, nonspontaneous, ΔG > 0** (p21, p22). Stated, then drawn as two bar diagrams.
9. **Reactions in a closed system reach equilibrium and then do no work; cells are open systems with a constant flow of materials, never at equilibrium; a catabolic pathway releases free energy in a series of reactions** (p23).
10. **Three hydroelectric systems: (a) isolated, ΔG < 0 then ΔG = 0 when levels match and the bulb dims; (b) open, a tap in and a drain out, ΔG < 0 all the time; (c) multistep open, three steps each ΔG < 0** (p24).

## Vocabulary

- **Free energy (G)**: energy of a system able to do work at uniform temperature and pressure.
- **ΔG, ΔH, ΔS, T**: change in free energy; change in enthalpy (total energy, heat content); change in entropy (disorder); temperature in Kelvin.
- **Spontaneous / nonspontaneous**: runs without outside energy input (not necessarily fast); does not.
- **Instability, stability, equilibrium**: tendency to change; the settled state; the state of maximum stability where ΔG = 0 and nothing changes net.
- **Exergonic, endergonic**: releases free energy (ΔG < 0); absorbs it (ΔG > 0).
- **Closed / isolated / open system**: no exchange; no exchange; exchanges materials (and energy) with surroundings.
- **Catabolic pathway**: series of reactions that releases free energy.

## Possible problems

- **p19 ink "ΔG > 0 = unspontaneous"**: fine in meaning; the book says "nonspontaneous". The Arabic beside it (تلقائي = spontaneous, غير تلقائي = nonspontaneous) is right.
- **p19 ink "ΔG = 0 في حالة اتزان" (in a state of equilibrium)**: right.
- **p19 Arabic "التغير في المحتوى الحراري" (change in heat content) for ΔH and "التغير بالإنتروبي" for ΔS**: fine; enthalpy is the heat content. The printed "change in total energy" is loose (enthalpy is not the same as total energy), so the voice says "enthalpy" with the slide's wording.
- **p20 ink "C6H12O6 → CO2 + H2O + heat" with "ATP"**: unbalanced and missing O2 as a reactant; glucose plus oxygen, with the energy captured partly as ATP and partly lost as heat. The first Arabic words ("go back to ...") are not fully legible. Not used as written; the voice says only that breaking down glucose is exergonic.
- **p20 ink "ΔG = 0" beside equilibrium**: right, with the meaning "no net change, no work".
- **p20 bullet "a process is spontaneous and can perform work only when moving toward equilibrium"**: right; at equilibrium the process is not spontaneous (ΔG = 0, not negative).
- **p23 "closed system" vs p24 "isolated"**: strictly a closed system exchanges energy but not matter and an isolated one neither; the slide's hydro figure (a) is isolated. Loose. See Errata.
- **p23 Arabic notes**: "in a closed system it reaches equilibrium and stops reacting" and "cells take materials in and out": right. Red circle on "metabolism" in "metabolism is never at equilibrium": right.
- **p24 bulb states**: in (a) the left tank still shows ΔG < 0 and the right ΔG = 0: right. (b) shows only ΔG < 0; the water in the top tank is held up by the tap, so the open system never reaches ΔG = 0.
- **p18 "when temperature and pressure are uniform"**: fine; ΔG < 0 as the test for spontaneity holds at constant T and P, which a cell is close to.
- **Spontaneous does not mean fast**: not on the slides; lesson 2 teaches it. Not repeated here beyond one clause avoided in the voice.

### Questions
