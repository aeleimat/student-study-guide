## Vocabulary

- **Polynucleotide**: a polymer of nucleotides; a nucleic acid.
- **Nucleotide**: one monomer of a nucleic acid: base + sugar + phosphate group.
- **Nucleoside**: a nucleotide without its phosphate group: base + sugar.
- **Nitrogenous base**: a ring-shaped molecule with nitrogen in its ring(s); A, G, C, T or U.
- **Pentose**: a sugar with five carbons.
- **Phosphate group**: a phosphorus atom bonded to four oxygens (one oxygen also bonds to the sugar's 5' carbon).
- **Purine**: a base with two fused rings (a 6-ring joined to a 5-ring): adenine, guanine.
- **Pyrimidine**: a base with a single 6-ring: cytosine, thymine, uracil.
- **Ribose**: the pentose in RNA, with an OH on carbon 2'.
- **Deoxyribose**: the pentose in DNA, with an H on carbon 2' (one oxygen fewer).
- **Prime (')**: the mark that separates the sugar's carbon numbers (1' to 5') from the atoms numbered in the base.
- **5' and 3'**: the carbon at which the phosphate attaches, and the carbon with the free OH.

## Plan

Dropped: structural formulas of the bases (atoms, NH2, O), the structure of the phosphate with charges, the methyl group of thymine, mono / di / triphosphates, the full diagram of the strand with 5' and 3' ends (lesson 4 teaches the backbone).

### Questions

- `q1` id `n-purine`, choice, "Which base is built from two fused rings, a six-sided ring joined to a five-sided ring?" Options: (a) cytosine; (b) thymine; (c) guanine (right); (d) uracil. Why: guanine and adenine are the purines. Wrong (a, b, d): these are pyrimidines, one six-sided ring.
- `q2` id `n-rna`, choice, "A nucleotide has uracil, ribose and one phosphate group. Where would you find it?" Options: (a) in DNA; (b) in RNA (right); (c) in both; (d) in neither. Why: uracil and ribose belong to RNA. Wrong (a): DNA has thymine and deoxyribose; (c): the sugar and base fit only RNA.
- `final1` ('Chapter practice'):
  - `p-nside` choice: "A nucleotide loses its phosphate group. What is left?" Right: a nucleoside (a base joined to a sugar). Wrong: a polynucleotide; a sugar only; a base pair.
  - `p-terms` grid, true/false: adenine is a purine (T); thymine has two fused rings (F, one six-sided ring); uracil takes the place of thymine in RNA (T); deoxyribose has one oxygen more than ribose (F, one fewer).
  - `p-carbons` blanks: "The base is attached to carbon ___′ of the sugar" 1; "The phosphate group is attached to carbon ___′" 5.
  - `p-sugar` choice: "What is the difference between ribose and deoxyribose?" Right: ribose has an OH on carbon 2′ where deoxyribose has only H. Wrong: ribose has one more carbon; deoxyribose has no oxygen at all; deoxyribose carries the phosphate.
